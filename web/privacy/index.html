Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026

Onda is a short-drama video app. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use Onda, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Handle

Onda does not currently require you to create an account. The app may handle the following information:

  • App activity, such as shows and episodes you view, playback progress, followed shows, onboarding preferences, and local notification preferences.
  • Device and technical information, such as device type, app version, IP address, network information, diagnostics, and performance data.
  • Advertising information related to rewarded ads shown through Google AdMob, including ad interactions and identifiers used by Google to provide and measure ads.
  • Notification permission status if you choose to enable notifications.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate and improve Onda, including to:

  • Load the catalog, posters, thumbnails, and video playback links.
  • Remember local watch progress, followed shows, and personalization preferences.
  • Show rewarded ads that unlock additional episodes.
  • Provide privacy choices where required by law or platform rules.
  • Monitor app and service reliability.

Advertising

Onda uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. Google may collect or receive information such as IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and performance data to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads. Onda uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and store ad privacy choices where required.

Onda does not currently request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or access the Identifier for Advertisers for cross-app tracking. If that changes, we will update this policy and the app's permission prompts.

Video and Backend Services

Onda uses backend services to provide catalog data and video playback. These services may process technical information such as IP address, request metadata, playback ticket requests, and device or browser information needed to deliver the app experience. Video, image, and playback assets may be delivered through services such as Cloudflare Stream, and app metadata may be served by hosted backend infrastructure.

Local Storage

Some preferences and watch-state information are stored locally on your device using iOS storage. This can include onboarding choices, followed shows, notification soft-ask state, completed shows, active playback progress, and ad privacy choices stored by Google's consent tools.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app preferences and watch-state information remain on your device until you delete the app, reset the related settings, or the app removes them as part of normal operation. Backend logs and service records may be retained for a limited period as needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve Onda, comply with legal obligations, and prevent misuse.

You can request deletion of personal information associated with Onda by contacting onda@vivant.llc. Because Onda does not currently require accounts, we may need information from you to locate any relevant records. Deleting the app removes locally stored app data from your device.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Onda may use notification permission status to show episode or app updates. You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Children

Onda is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can manage certain choices in the app and in iOS Settings, including notification permissions and, where shown, ad privacy choices. Where required, Onda provides a privacy choices entry point that lets you update or revoke ad-related consent choices. You can also delete the app to remove locally stored app data from your device.

Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Onda, including video delivery, backend hosting, analytics or diagnostics, and advertising partners. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Onda changes. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to onda@vivant.llc.
