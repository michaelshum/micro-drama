Support

Help for the Onda short-drama iOS app.

If you need help with Onda, contact us at onda@vivant.llc. Include the device you are using, your iOS version if you know it, and a short description of the issue.

Common Questions

How do episode unlocks work?

Some episodes may require a rewarded ad before viewing. Ad availability can vary by region, network conditions, privacy choices, and ad provider availability.

Why is an ad not loading?

Check your internet connection and try again. If rewarded ads are temporarily unavailable, wait a moment and retry. Availability is not guaranteed for every session.

How do I manage notifications?

You can manage Onda notification permissions in the iOS Settings app under Notifications.

How do I manage privacy choices?

Where available, Onda provides ad privacy choices in the app. You can also review our Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I request data deletion?

Email onda@vivant.llc. Because Onda does not currently require accounts, we may need information from you to locate any relevant records. Deleting the app removes locally stored app data from your device.
